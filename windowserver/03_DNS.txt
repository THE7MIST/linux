===============================
IMPORTANT EXAM NOTES
===============================

1. Take screenshots of:
   - DNS Role installed
   - Forward Lookup Zone
   - Reverse Lookup Zone
   - A Record (master, www)
   - PTR Record

2. Common mistakes:
   - DNS not pointing to itself (127.0.0.1 or server IP)
   - Forgot PTR checkbox
   - Wrong zone name
   - Typo in IP or domain

3. If resolution fails:
   - Restart DNS service
   - Flush cache → ipconfig /flushdns
   - Recheck A + PTR mapping
